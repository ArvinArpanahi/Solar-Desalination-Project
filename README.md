## Executive Summary

This project presents the development of a next-generation desalination infrastructure based on Concentrated Solar Power (CSP – solar tower technology), integrated with thermal desalination and mineral recovery systems.

The platform is designed to transform seawater into multiple high-value outputs:

- Freshwater for municipal and industrial use
- Thermal energy for internal consumption and potential export
- Industrial minerals recovered from brine

This project is positioned as a scalable infrastructure platform for coastal deployment, with strong long-term potential for industrial integration, environmental sustainability, and regional resource security.

---

## Strategic Rationale

The project responds to several structural regional challenges:

- Water scarcity
- Growing industrial and urban demand
- Dependence on fossil-fuel-based desalination
- Pressure on long-term sustainability

By combining renewable thermal energy, desalination, and mineral recovery, the project creates a more resilient and economically attractive model than conventional desalination systems.

## Core Technology

### CSP Solar Tower

![Solar Tower Close](images/csp-tower-close.jpg)

Key components include:

- Heliostat mirror field
- Central receiver tower
- Operating temperature range: 500–1000°C
- Molten salt thermal storage
- Continuous and stabilized operation capability

---

### System Operation

![CSP Overview](images/csp-tower-overview.gif)

Solar radiation is concentrated onto the tower receiver, generating thermal energy that can be used directly in desalination or stored for continuous operation.

---

### Desalination Integration

Primary technologies considered:

- MED (Multi-Effect Distillation)  
  Optimized for thermal integration and lower operational intensity

- MSF (Multi-Stage Flash)  
  Proven industrial-scale technology with robust performance

The final technology mix depends on site conditions, water chemistry, scale, and investment strategy.

---

### Mineral Recovery

Potential outputs from brine include:

- Sodium Chloride (NaCl)
- Potassium Sulfate (K₂SO₄)
- Sodium Sulfate
- Magnesium salts
- Future lithium extraction potential, subject to feasibility

This strengthens the business case by converting a waste stream into a potential value stream.
