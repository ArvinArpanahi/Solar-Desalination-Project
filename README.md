# 🌊 Solar Desalination & Mineral Recovery Project

🚀 A large-scale solar-powered desalination and mineral recovery project designed for sustainable water production and industrial resource generation in the Persian Gulf region.

---

## 📌 Executive Summary

This project proposes the development of an integrated coastal complex utilizing Concentrated Solar Power (CSP – solar tower technology) to generate high-temperature thermal energy for desalination and mineral extraction.

The system converts seawater into multiple valuable outputs, including fresh water, clean energy, and industrial minerals, creating a multi-revenue infrastructure model.

---

## 🧩 Overview

The project is based on a circular heliostat field focusing solar radiation onto a central tower receiver. The generated thermal energy is used to power desalination systems and support continuous operation through thermal storage.

Key characteristics:

- Solar-powered operation (low carbon footprint)
- High efficiency thermal energy generation
- Modular and scalable design
- Integration with desalination and mineral recovery systems

---

## ⚙️ Core Technology

### ☀️ CSP Solar Tower

- Circular heliostat mirror field
- Central receiver tower
- Operating temperature: 500–1000°C
- Thermal storage (molten salts)
- Continuous operation capability

---

### 💧 Desalination System

Recommended technologies:

#### MED (Multi-Effect Distillation)
- High efficiency with thermal energy
- Lower operational costs
- Best integration with CSP

#### MSF (Multi-Stage Flash)
- Proven large-scale technology
- Robust but higher energy consumption

---

### 🧂 Mineral Recovery

From desalination brine:

- Sodium Chloride (NaCl)
- Potassium Sulfate (K₂SO₄)
- Sodium Sulfate
- Magnesium salts
- Future potential: Lithium extraction
