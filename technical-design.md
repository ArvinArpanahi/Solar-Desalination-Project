# ⚙️ Technical Concept – Solar Desalination & Mineral Recovery System

## 🔬 Technical Position

This project is presented as a large-scale infrastructure concept, not as an off-the-shelf consumer device.

The concept is based on the integration of:

- Concentrated Solar Power (CSP) / solar tower technology
- Thermal desalination systems
- Mineral recovery from desalination brine

The objective is to create a multi-output industrial platform capable of producing fresh water and commercially valuable mineral by-products from seawater.

---

## ☀️ Core Energy Concept

The energy backbone of the project is based on solar tower / CSP architecture.

In this configuration:

- A circular heliostat field reflects solar radiation
- Solar energy is concentrated on a central receiver tower
- High-temperature thermal energy is produced
- The generated heat is then used to support desalination and related thermal processes

This is a globally recognized technology class with operating examples in multiple countries.

---

## 💧 Desalination Concept

The project is intended to use thermal desalination technologies compatible with concentrated solar heat.

Most relevant options include:

### 1. Multi-Effect Distillation (MED)
- Higher thermal efficiency
- Better integration with solar heat
- Lower operational intensity compared with some alternatives

### 2. Multi-Stage Flash (MSF)
- Well-established at large scale
- Technically robust
- Higher thermal demand

At this stage, the project does not claim a finalized technology selection.
The final configuration should be determined through feasibility study, engineering design, and partner consultation.

---

## 🧂 Mineral Recovery Concept

A key differentiator of this concept is the treatment of desalination brine as a resource stream, not only as waste.

Potential recoverable products may include:

- Sodium chloride (industrial salt)
- Potassium sulfate
- Sodium sulfate
- Magnesium compounds
- Other mineral products depending on feedwater chemistry and process selection

The exact mineral product mix depends on:
- feedwater composition
- process design
- economic viability
- downstream market access

---

## 📊 Capacity Range (Concept Level)

At concept stage, a realistic base configuration is:

- Fresh water output: 100,000 to 300,000 m³/day
- Equivalent to: 0.1 to 0.3 million m³/day

Higher output levels are possible, but would depend on:
- project scale
- number of units
- modular deployment
- multi-site expansion

For this reason, capacity above the base range should be presented as future scalable potential, not as initial guaranteed output.
