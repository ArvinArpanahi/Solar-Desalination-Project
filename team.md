# Team & Project Structure

## Overview

The Solar Desalination & Mineral Recovery Project is structured as a large-scale industrial infrastructure initiative requiring a multidisciplinary team with expertise in energy systems, desalination technologies, engineering, finance, and project execution.

The team is designed not only to develop the concept but to successfully deliver, operate, and scale the project.
