# Operations Plan

## Overview

This document defines the operational structure of the Solar Desalination & Mineral Recovery Project, outlining how the system will function on a daily, monthly, and long-term basis.

The operational model is designed to ensure:

- Continuous production of water and energy  
- Stable system performance  
- Efficient resource utilization  
- Cost-effective long-term operation  
- Scalability for future expansion  

This is not a passive infrastructure project; it is an active industrial system requiring coordinated technical, logistical, and operational management.

---

## Operational Architecture

The project is structured across four primary operational layers:

### 1. Energy Layer (CSP System)

Responsible for:
- Solar energy capture via heliostat field  
- Thermal energy generation in central tower  
- Energy storage via molten salt systems  
- Continuous heat supply for desalination  

👉 Role:
Provides the core energy backbone of the entire system.

---

### 2. Water Production Layer (Desalination)

Responsible for:
- Seawater intake  
- Thermal desalination (MED/MSF)  
- Freshwater production and conditioning  
- Brine output management  

👉 Role:
Converts seawater into usable freshwater.

---

### 3. Resource Recovery Layer (Minerals)

Responsible for:
- Brine processing  
- Chemical separation  
- Mineral extraction and drying  
- Product handling and storage  

👉 Role:
Transforms waste streams into revenue-generating outputs.

---

### 4. Infrastructure & Logistics Layer

Responsible for:
- Transport of materials and outputs  
- Port connectivity  
- Storage facilities  
- Internal logistics coordination  

👉 Role:
Ensures operational continuity and efficient supply chain movement.

---
